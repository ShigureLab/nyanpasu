# Review workflow

The parent owns review coverage and cross-scope reconciliation. reviewer.md supplies investigation criteria; review-output.md owns eligibility, progress display and publication. The injected task-control instructions own operation schemas, waiting, retries and child lifecycle.

## Establish or resume the work

Apply review-output.md's eligibility check at every entry. A CI-only turn follows ci-followup.md alone. Otherwise, read current PR requirements, requests and existing review evidence; use `pr view --after <previous fetched_at>` when available and expand relevant discussions. An unchanged head does not dispose of an outstanding request.

Use github-conversation to acknowledge a new review, human request or substantive feedback with one fitting reaction on the original item, while preserving the existing silence rules.

Verify the worktree's pinned head and merge-base. When the turn provides changes since the last verified review, start with that head-to-head range and recheck its open findings and other remaining questions. Trace affected contracts into unchanged callers and revisit prior evidence when the current requirements, requests or behavior warrant it. Use the full pinned direct-base diff when no verified comparison is supplied. Keep the current direct-base inventory for scope and publication. Distinguish new regressions, earlier misses, known findings and pre-existing base defects. Reuse completed coverage when the delta, requirements and outstanding requests allow it. Apply review-output.md's notification rules after reviewing a new delta as well.

Read `review-inventory` for the pinned changed paths. Use those exact path tokens to assign work. For stacks, use the direct-base diff and attribute inherited issues to their introducing layer. Establish the dashboard under review-output.md. Refresh CI under ci-followup.md alongside code review, without waiting for pending checks or duplicating broad tests.

Reconcile active and delivered children against the current range and requirements. Cancel obsolete work and retain its evidence at the original identity; do not relabel stale results. Resume remaining investigations under the same process below.

## Investigate and reconcile

Track material questions, owners, evidence and gaps in `review-plan.json`, matching candidates by root cause and affected contract with canonical finding IDs. Assign concrete operation/reuse comparisons across related entry points to `purpose: redundancy-review`. When configuration changes role composition or resource ownership, assign a separate `purpose: startup-mode-contract` question tracing normalization, registration, allocation and final consumption, including affected paths that skip startup stages. Supply relevant documentation and caller contracts; a guard alone does not establish intentional support policy. Let investigators choose candidates across changed paths, including shared lifecycle boundaries. Continue checking affected behavior and global interactions meanwhile. A small cohesive edit can stay with the parent. Add a convention investigator only for an independent question with a concrete rule source. Assign one owner per question; they may share files. Follow up on unresolved evidence and affected contracts.

Use managed task control. The plugin supplies child roles and criteria; omit `developer_instructions`. Put the question, contract sources, evidence and constraints in `prompt`; independent design uses design-comparison.md's structured requirements instead. Supply evidence with revision, command/job, result and limitations; repeat a check only for a remaining uncertainty.

Code-review children return conclusions, checked paths, evidence and gaps; the parent owns global coverage and reconciles overlapping or contradictory proposals. Group connected PR comparisons in one child; shared names or files alone do not establish overlap. Split and comparison advice do not own review coverage or reopen an otherwise unchanged completed review. Reuse matching active or frozen analyses only while their ranges and material requirements remain valid, citing them in the plan.

When the current turn supplies a Codenip skill path and the pinned diff has changed files, dispatch one managed `purpose: codenip` coordinator with `inputs.review_files` and the current `inputs.pr_body` when available. Sol selects rules for the assigned diff and each selected Luna worker decides applicability. Track the coordinator and verify its advice before publishing under review-output.md. A Codenip gap does not close or block ordinary review coverage.

Read delivered evidence and reconcile each assigned question as supported, rejected with counterevidence, or unresolved in the existing plan. Preserve independently supported maintenance findings in delivered conclusions; adjacent findings do not close an assigned question. For high-risk candidates or conflicting evidence, obtain a fresh scoped investigator's falsification before accepting the claim. Supply the claim, sources and a question that could disprove its trigger, contract or consequence, without prescribing a verdict. Reuse valid evidence; straightforward supported findings need no repeated counter-review. Agreement alone adds no proof.

Continue useful parent work while children run, then use task-control's asynchronous wait. Reconcile each delivered result and continue remaining needed work. A failed or cancelled essential child leaves a gap to recover or report. If successive rounds restate symptoms or trade one symptom for another, revisit the root cause, sourced requirements and responsibility boundary; seek a discriminating example or missing decision.

## Deliver and finish

Follow review-output.md to publish supported findings as they become useful and preserve progress before long experiments or waits. Its dashboard stages describe delivered coverage; they do not prescribe investigation rounds.

Finish when coverage of the pinned changes and the material questions have supported conclusions. Keep essential evidence gaps visible. Reaching a round count does not justify closure. Reconcile optional analyses under review-output.md without making their outcome a correctness blocker, then follow its final verification and publication protocol.
